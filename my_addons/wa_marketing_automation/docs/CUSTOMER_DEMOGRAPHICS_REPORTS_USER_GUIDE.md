# Customer Demographics Reports - User Guide

## Overview

The Customer Demographics Reports provide comprehensive insights into your customer base using age demographics, purchase behavior analysis, and marketing engagement metrics. These reports help you understand customer segments, identify opportunities, and make data-driven marketing decisions.

## Accessing the Reports

**Navigation Path:** `WhatsApp Marketing Automation > Reports`

**Available Reports:**
1. **Customer Demographics Analysis** - Main comprehensive report
2. **Age Distribution Analysis** - Visual age group breakdown 
3. **RFM Analysis** - Behavioral segmentation
4. **Churn Risk Analysis** - At-risk customer identification

---

## 1. Customer Demographics Analysis

**Path:** `WhatsApp Marketing Automation > Reports > Customer Demographics Analysis`

### What This Report Shows

This is the main report that combines multiple data dimensions:
- **Age Demographics** - Customer age groups and birth dates
- **Geographic Information** - Country, state, city distribution  
- **Purchase Behavior** - RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis
- **Customer Scoring** - Overall customer value score (1-10)
- **Marketing Engagement** - WhatsApp campaign interactions

### View Modes

#### **Pivot View (Recommended for Analysis)**
- **Best for:** Cross-tabulation and data drilling
- **How to use:** 
  - Drag age groups to rows, recency segments to columns
  - Add measures like customer count, total spent, average order value
  - Double-click cells to drill down into specific segments

#### **Graph View**
- **Best for:** Visual trend analysis
- **Available charts:** Bar charts, pie charts, line graphs
- **How to switch:** Use the chart type buttons in the toolbar

#### **List View**
- **Best for:** Detailed customer-by-customer analysis
- **Features:** Sortable columns, search functionality, bulk operations
- **Optional columns:** Show/hide columns using the column selector

#### **Form View**
- **Best for:** Individual customer deep-dive
- **Access:** Click any customer record from list view
- **Features:** Complete customer profile with all demographic and behavioral data

### Key Metrics Explained

#### **Age Demographics**
- **Age Groups:** 18-25, 26-35, 36-50, 51-65, 65+, Unknown
- **Use cases:** 
  - Target age-specific marketing campaigns
  - Analyze product preferences by age
  - Plan inventory based on demographic trends

#### **RFM Analysis Components**

**Recency (How recently they purchased):**
- **Active:** 0-30 days since last purchase
- **Recent:** 31-90 days 
- **Dormant:** 91-365 days
- **Lost:** 365+ days
- **New:** No purchase history

**Frequency (How often they purchase):**
- **High:** 10+ orders
- **Medium:** 5-9 orders
- **Low:** 2-4 orders
- **One-time:** 1 order
- **None:** No orders

**Monetary (How much they spend):**
- **High Value:** Top 20% of spenders
- **Medium Value:** Middle 60% of spenders  
- **Low Value:** Bottom 20% of spenders
- **No Value:** No purchases

#### **Customer Score (1-10 Scale)**
Calculated by combining:
- **Recency Score:** 1-3 points (recent purchases = higher score)
- **Frequency Score:** 1-3 points (more orders = higher score)  
- **Monetary Score:** 1-4 points (higher spending = higher score)

**Score Interpretation:**
- **8-10:** VIP customers (high value, frequent, recent)
- **6-7:** Good customers (solid engagement)
- **4-5:** Average customers (room for improvement)
- **1-3:** At-risk or low-value customers

### Using Filters Effectively

#### **Quick Filters (Left Panel)**
- **Active Customers:** Recent purchasers (0-30 days)
- **Dormant Customers:** Need re-engagement (91-365 days)
- **High Value Customers:** Top spenders
- **Age Groups:** Target specific demographics
- **Churn Risk:** High-value customers who haven't purchased recently

#### **Advanced Search**
- **Search by name:** Find specific customers
- **Combine filters:** e.g., "High Value" + "Age 26-35" + "Dormant"
- **Custom domains:** Create complex filter conditions

#### **Group By Options**
- **Age Group:** See trends across demographics
- **Country:** Geographic analysis
- **Recency Segment:** Understand purchase timing patterns
- **Customer Score:** Identify value tiers

## 3. RFM Analysis

**Path:** `WhatsApp Marketing Automation > Reports > RFM Analysis`

### What This Report Shows
Behavioral segmentation based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary analysis.

### Customer Segments & Actions

#### **Champions (Recent + High Frequency + High Value)**
- **Profile:** Your best customers
- **Action:** Reward with exclusive offers, early access, VIP treatment

#### **Loyal Customers (Recent + High Frequency + Medium Value)** 
- **Profile:** Engaged customers with growth potential
- **Action:** Upsell premium products, increase order value

#### **Potential Loyalists (Recent + Low Frequency + Any Value)**
- **Profile:** New customers showing promise
- **Action:** Nurture with onboarding campaigns, build loyalty

#### **At Risk (Not Recent + High Frequency + High Value)**
- **Profile:** Previously valuable customers becoming inactive  
- **Action:** Win-back campaigns, special incentives, surveys

#### **Cannot Lose Them (Lost + High Frequency + High Value)**
- **Profile:** Best customers who've stopped purchasing
- **Action:** Urgent intervention, personal outreach, exclusive deals

#### **Hibernating (Lost + Low Frequency + Any Value)**
- **Profile:** Inactive customers
- **Action:** Re-engagement campaigns, product recommendations

### Using RFM for Marketing Automation
1. **Segment Selection:** Choose RFM segments for targeted campaigns
2. **Message Personalization:** Craft messages based on customer behavior
3. **Campaign Timing:** Contact frequency based on engagement level
4. **WhatsApp Integration:** Use segments for automated WhatsApp campaigns

## 4. Churn Risk Analysis

**Path:** `WhatsApp Marketing Automation > Reports > Churn Risk Analysis`

### What This Report Shows
High-value customers at risk of churning (stopping purchases).

### Risk Criteria
Customers who are:
- **High previous value** (were good customers)
- **High previous frequency** (used to purchase often)  
- **Now dormant or lost** (haven't purchased recently)

### Business Applications
- **Proactive Retention:** Contact at-risk customers before they churn
- **Win-back Campaigns:** Design specific retention offers
- **Root Cause Analysis:** Understand why valuable customers leave
- **Resource Prioritization:** Focus retention efforts on high-value customers

### Action Steps
1. **Identify:** Use the report to find at-risk customers
2. **Prioritize:** Sort by customer score to focus on most valuable
3. **Engage:** Create targeted WhatsApp campaigns
4. **Monitor:** Track response rates and retention success

## Understanding the Data Fields

### Customer Information
- **Name:** Customer display name
- **Age:** Calculated from birth date
- **Age Group:** Categorized age range
- **Date of Birth:** Customer's birth date (if available)

### Geographic Data
- **Country:** Customer's country
- **State:** Customer's state/province  
- **City:** Customer's city
- **Company:** Associated company (if applicable)

### Purchase Behavior
- **Last Purchase Date:** Most recent order date
- **Days Since Last Purchase:** Recency calculation
- **Total Orders:** Lifetime order count
- **Total Spent:** Lifetime revenue from customer
- **Average Order Value:** Total spent ÷ Total orders

### Calculated Segments
- **Recency Segment:** Based on days since last purchase
- **Frequency Segment:** Based on total order count
- **Monetary Segment:** Based on total spending percentiles
- **Customer Score:** Composite RFM score (1-10)

### Marketing Metrics
- **WhatsApp Campaigns Sent:** Number of campaigns received
- **WhatsApp Campaigns Opened:** Number of campaigns engaged with
- **WhatsApp Engagement Rate:** Open rate percentage

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### No Data Showing
- **Check Filters:** Remove all filters to see full dataset
- **Verify Customer Data:** Ensure customers exist in the system
- **Database Permissions:** Confirm user has access to customer data

### Missing Age Information
- **Birth Date Entry:** Add birth dates to customer profiles
- **Data Import:** Bulk import birth dates if available
- **Age appears as "Unknown":** Customer birth date not set

### Incorrect RFM Segments
- **Order Status:** Verify sale orders are confirmed/done status
- **Date Ranges:** Check if order dates are within expected ranges
- **Currency Issues:** Ensure monetary values are in correct currency

### Performance Issues
- **Large Datasets:** Use filters to limit scope
- **Browser Cache:** Clear cache if reports load slowly
- **Database Optimization:** Consider indexing for large customer bases
